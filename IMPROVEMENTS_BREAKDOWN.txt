                        USER EXPERIENCE FLOW
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

BEFORE:
1. User navigates to app
2. Enters first number
3. Enters second number
4. Selects operation
5. Clicks calculate
6. Sees result
7. No further tracking
⏱️ Total: 7 clicks, no history

AFTER:
1. User navigates to app (sees beautiful interface)
2. Enters first number
3. Presses Enter (or Tab to next field)
4. Enters second number
5. Presses Enter or clicks button (or uses quick ops)
6. Sees result instantly
7. Result shown + added to history
8. Can copy result, try another calculation
9. History visible on right panel
⏱️ Total: 3-5 interactions, full history tracking
